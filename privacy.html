Privacy Policy – Hammer Smash

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Hammer Smash ("the game") is made by Pionix LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses on Android and iPhone/iPad, and why. If you have a question, write to pionixllc@gmail.com.

What we do not collect

The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and we do not run our own servers that receive information about you.

Your game progress

Your progress (levels, coins, gems, weapons and settings) is saved only on your own device. It is not sent to us. Deleting the game deletes this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads, including optional video ads you can choose to watch for rewards. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iPhone and iPad, advertising ID on Android), your IP address and approximate location derived from it, device information, and how you interact with ads.

  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks for your permission before your advertising identifier can be used to track you across apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised with that identifier.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the PRIVACY button in the game's menu.
  • You can also limit ad tracking in your device's settings.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's privacy policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In-app purchases

Gem packs can be bought in the game. Payments are handled entirely by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made so the gems can be added to your game.

Internet connection

The game checks whether your device is online, because ads and purchases need the internet. This check does not send personal information to us.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
